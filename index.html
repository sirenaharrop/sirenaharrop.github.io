About Me

I am currently a Predoctoral Researcher (RSA) with the Political Methodology Lab at MIT.

I recently obtained a Master of Public Policy from UC Berkeley. Previously, I completed a Bachelor of Commerce with honours in Business and Computer Science at UBC and worked as a software engineer.

My academic interests are primarily at the intersection of frontier technologies and society. I am particularly interested in understanding how new technologies affect labor, migration, and livelihoods on a global level.

Looking ahead, I will be applying to PhD programs where I can continue exploring these questions in greater depth.
